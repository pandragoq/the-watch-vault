Rolex

Explorer 36

124270

The Explorer traces its name to 1953, when Rolex watches accompanied the first confirmed ascent of Mount Everest, and the model has stayed true to that tool-watch brief ever since: a simple, highly legible three-hand dial with no date. Reference 124270, introduced in 2021, returned the line to a 36mm Oystersteel case after several years of 39mm-only production, pairing it with the in-house calibre 3230 and a Chromalight-lumed 3-6-9 dial. It is one of the few current steel sports Rolex models whose secondary-market price now sits close to its official retail price.
