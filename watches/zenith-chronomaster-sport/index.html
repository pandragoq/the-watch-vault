Zenith

Chronomaster Sport

03.3100.3600/69.M3100

Launched in 2021 to mark 50 years since the debut of the automatic El Primero chronograph, the Chronomaster Sport reworks the tricolour counters and pump pushers of Zenith's vintage A386-era chronographs into a modern 41mm steel sports-watch case with an integrated bracelet and black ceramic bezel. Its defining trick is the El Primero 3600 movement, which reads to 1/10th of a second via a central chronograph hand that completes one full rotation every ten seconds against the bezel's graduated scale. This white-dial, steel-bracelet reference is the model's signature configuration and the version most associated with the line since launch.
