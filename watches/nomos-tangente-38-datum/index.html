NOMOS Glashütte

Tangente 38 Datum

130

The Tangente is NOMOS's founding design from 1992, a minimalist dress watch whose thin lugs, railway-track minute scale and Bauhaus-style numerals became the brand's signature look. This 38mm version with date (reference 130) is built around the in-house DUW 4101, NOMOS's first manual-wind caliber with a patented, large-format date display and a sapphire display case back, replacing the earlier ETA/Peseux-based Beta movement used in older Tangente Datum references.
