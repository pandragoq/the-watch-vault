Omega

Seamaster Diver 300M

210.30.42.20.03.001

The Seamaster Diver 300M has been Omega's flagship dive watch since 1993, when it was designed with a distinctive wave-embossed dial and helium escape valve and later gained global recognition as James Bond's watch of choice from GoldenEye onward. This 2018-generation reference pairs a polished blue ceramic dial and unidirectional ceramic bezel with the in-house Co-Axial Master Chronometer Calibre 8800, giving it METAS-certified precision and strong resistance to magnetism on top of its 300m dive-rated water resistance.
