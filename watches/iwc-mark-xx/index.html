IWC Schaffhausen

Pilot's Watch Mark XX

IW328201“Mark XX”

Launched in 2022, the Mark XX is the latest chapter in IWC's Mark-series pilot's watches that trace back to the World War II-era Mark 11 issued to the Royal Air Force. Skipping the number XIX, it replaced the long-running Mark XVIII with a trimmer 40mm x 10.8mm case, a shorter lug-to-lug, water resistance increased to 100m, and the manufacture's 5-day automatic calibre 32111, making it the brand's benchmark entry-level tool watch.
