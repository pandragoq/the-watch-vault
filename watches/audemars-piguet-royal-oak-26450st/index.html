Audemars Piguet

Royal Oak Selfwinding Chronograph 38mm

26450ST.OO.1356ST.01-B
WISHLIST

Gérald Genta's 1972 Royal Oak created the luxury steel sports-watch archetype, and this ref. 26450ST.OO.1356ST.01-B, unveiled in February 2026, is the renewed 38mm Royal Oak Selfwinding Chronograph, the most compact chronograph in AP's current line. It replaces the outgoing Frédéric Piguet-derived movement with AP's new in-house calibre 6401 -- a column-wheel, vertical-clutch automatic -- and adds a sapphire exhibition caseback for the first time on this case size, while keeping the familiar octagonal-bezel steel case, integrated bracelet and blue Grande Tapisserie dial.
