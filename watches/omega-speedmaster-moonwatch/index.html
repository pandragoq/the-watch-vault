Omega

Speedmaster Moonwatch Professional

310.30.42.50.01.001“Moonwatch”

Buzz Aldrin wore a Speedmaster on the lunar surface in 1969 after NASA qualified it as the only chronograph to survive its extravehicular-activity testing, and Omega has kept the model in continuous production ever since, cementing the 'Moonwatch' nickname. This reference, introduced in January 2021, keeps the manual-wind chronograph's classic asymmetrical steel case, hesalite (acrylic) crystal and printed dial while upgrading the engine to the Co-Axial Master Chronometer Calibre 3861 for better accuracy and magnetic resistance. It is one of the very few Master Chronometer-certified movements still assembled and finished largely by hand.
