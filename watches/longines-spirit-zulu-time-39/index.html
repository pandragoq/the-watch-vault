Longines

Spirit Zulu Time

L3.802.4.53.6

The Spirit Zulu Time revives the layout of Longines' 1925 dual-time-zone pilot's watch, taking its name from the 'Zulu' designation aviators use for universal time. Launched in 42mm in 2020 and shrunk to this more wearable 39mm case in 2023, it pairs a COSC-certified, silicon-balance in-house GMT calibre with a ceramic-insert bezel, making it one of the more technically serious watches in Longines' current lineup. This reference is the core black-dial, steel-bracelet version of the 39mm model.
