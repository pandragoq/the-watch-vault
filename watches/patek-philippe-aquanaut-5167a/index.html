5167A-001“Aquanaut”

Introduced in 2007 to mark the Aquanaut line's tenth anniversary, the 5167A reworked the original 1997 sports watch with a bolder embossed dial pattern, a redesigned strap that curves into the case, and a slightly enlarged 40.8mm steel case. Its automatic caliber 26-330 S C descends from the long-running 324 S C and adds a date display and sweep seconds. Prized as an everyday luxury sports watch, the steel 5167A now trades well above its official list price on the secondary market.
