Rolex

Air-King

126900
OWNED

Introduced in 1945 as part of Rolex's post-war 'Air' series honouring Royal Air Force pilots, the Air-King is the brand's dedicated aviation watch, valued for legibility and steady chronometric performance rather than any diving or exploration specialty. Reference 126900, launched in 2022, reworked the model with a case shared with the Milgauss family, adding crown guards and a modernised 40mm Oystersteel case around the in-house calibre 3230, while keeping the signature oversized 3-6-9 numerals and railway-style minute track inspired by Rolex's Bloodhound SSC land-speed-record chronograph. As of July 2026 it remains Rolex's only current-production Air-King reference, positioned as one of the brand's more accessible steel sports watches.
