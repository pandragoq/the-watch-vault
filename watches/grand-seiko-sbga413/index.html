Grand Seiko

Heritage Collection Shunbun

SBGA413“Shunbun”

Part of Grand Seiko's Heritage "Four Seasons" series, the Shunbun reinterprets the 1967 62GS case in lightweight titanium and pairs it with a textured pink dial evoking Hana-Ikada — cherry blossom petals drifting on a river just after the spring equinox. It is powered by the 9R65 Spring Drive caliber, which combines a mechanical mainspring with a quartz-regulated glide-motion seconds hand for the smooth sweep Spring Drive is known for.
