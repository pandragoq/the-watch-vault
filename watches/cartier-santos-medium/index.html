Santos de Cartier Medium

WSSA0029“Santos Medium”

Louis Cartier designed the original Santos in 1904 for the aviator Alberto Santos-Dumont, who needed to read the time mid-flight without letting go of the controls — making it one of the earliest purpose-built men's wristwatches. This medium steel model belongs to the 2018 relaunch of the line, which kept the square bezel, exposed screws, and blued sword hands of the original while adding the tool-free QuickSwitch strap system and SmartLink bracelet sizing. It remains one of Cartier's best-selling steel sports watches, valued for combining early-aviation heritage with everyday practicality.
