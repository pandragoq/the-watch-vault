Audemars Piguet

Royal Oak Jumbo Extra-Thin

16202ST.OO.1240ST.01“Jumbo, 50th Anniversary”
Discontinued

Unveiled in January 2022 to mark the 50th anniversary of Gérald Genta's original 1972 Royal Oak 'Jumbo' ref. 5402, the 16202ST.OO.1240ST.01 revives the extra-thin 39mm steel case with a deep 'Bleu Nuit, Nuage 50' Petite Tapisserie dial and a rose-gold anniversary oscillating weight visible through the caseback. It introduced Audemars Piguet's new in-house automatic calibre 7121, replacing the long-serving 2121/3120 family, but was produced only during the anniversary year before being succeeded as the ongoing steel Jumbo by the visually near-identical ref. 16202ST.OO.1240ST.02. Like other in-production steel Royal Oak sports models, tight allocation means it continues to trade well above its original list price on the secondary market.
