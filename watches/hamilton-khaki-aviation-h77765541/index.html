Hamilton

Khaki Aviation X-Wind Day Date Auto 45mm

H77765541“X-Wind”
WISHLIST

Hamilton's Khaki Aviation line traces back to the Lancaster-founded brand's WWII-era navigation and observation watches for the US military, and its X-Wind sub-family, co-developed with aerobatic champion Nicolas Ivanoff and launched in 2005, adds a functioning bidirectional rotating bezel that lets a pilot dial in wind speed and read off the resulting crosswind drift angle before landing. Reference H77765541 is the automatic Day-Date version of the X-Wind: a 45mm steel case with large SuperLumiNova-filled Arabic numerals and a 9 o'clock day/date window for rapid legibility, powered by Hamilton's in-house H-30 automatic caliber with an 80-hour power reserve. It is one of relatively few series-produced watches with a genuinely functional aviation slide-rule bezel rather than a purely decorative one.
