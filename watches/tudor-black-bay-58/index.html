Tudor

Black Bay Fifty-Eight

M79030N-0001“BB58”
Discontinued

Launched in 2018 to mark Tudor's 58th anniversary, the Black Bay Fifty-Eight took the brand's modern Black Bay dive watch and shrank it to a genuinely vintage-proportioned 39mm case, answering years of complaints that the standard 41-43mm Black Bay wore too large. Reference M79030N-0001 paired a gilt-accented black dial with a riveted steel bracelet and Tudor's in-house, COSC-certified MT5402 movement, quickly making it one of the most sought-after modern sport watches for offering vintage Submariner-style looks at a fraction of Rolex pricing. Tudor retired this first-generation case and dial in mid-2026 when it redesigned the Black Bay 58 line around a thinner case and the newer Calibre MT5400-U.
