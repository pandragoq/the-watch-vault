IWC Schaffhausen

Pilot's Watch Chronograph

IW378004“Pilot's Chronograph 43”
WISHLIST

IWC's Pilot's Watch Chronograph traces its design language back to the brand's first Flieger-style chronograph of 1994, itself a descendant of the antimagnetic Mark 11 navigation watches IWC built for the Royal Air Force from 1948. Reference IW378004 is the steel-bracelet, sunray-brushed blue-dial version of the 43mm generation introduced in 2022, powered by IWC's in-house column-wheel chronograph calibre 69385 with a 46-hour power reserve. Oversized luminous Arabic numerals, a screw-in crown and a sapphire display back carry forward the high-legibility cockpit-instrument aesthetic that has defined IWC's pilot's watches for decades.
