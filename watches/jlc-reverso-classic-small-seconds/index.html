Reverso Classic Monoface Small Seconds

Q3868520“Reverso Classic Small Seconds”

Introduced in October 2025, the Reverso Classic Monoface Small Seconds is Jaeger-LeCoultre's slimmed-down update to the manually wound, single-dial Reverso first launched in 1931 as a rugged reversible watch for polo players in colonial India. It replaces the previous-generation Q2438520/Q2438522 models with a slightly smaller 40.1 x 24.4mm case, a new coin-stack guilloché dial pattern, and the same hand-wound Calibre 822, while keeping the swivelling case back free for personalised engraving. It is one of the more accessible entry points into JLC's Reverso Classic line, aimed at collectors who want the brand's Art Deco proportions without a date window or extra complication on the dial.
