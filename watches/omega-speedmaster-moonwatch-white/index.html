Speedmaster Moonwatch Professional

310.30.42.50.04.001“Panda Moonwatch”
WISHLIST

The Speedmaster earned its 'Moonwatch' nickname after NASA qualified it as the only chronograph to survive extravehicular-activity testing and Buzz Aldrin wore one on the lunar surface in 1969, a legacy that traces back to the model's earlier life as a dashboard-tested pilot's and racing chronograph. Reference 310.30.42.50.04.001, added to the regular Moonwatch Professional collection in 2024, was the first series-production Moonwatch with a glossy white lacquered dial and sapphire crystals front and back rather than the traditional hesalite, paired with the manual-wind Co-Axial Master Chronometer Calibre 3861. It keeps the model's classic asymmetrical steel case and black tachymeter bezel while giving the three-register 'panda' dial layout a cleaner, dressier read than the black-dial original.
