Hamilton

Khaki Field Mechanical 38mm

H69439931

Hamilton's Khaki Field Mechanical revives the utilitarian field watches the Lancaster-founded brand supplied to the US and other militaries through the mid-20th century, before Hamilton's watchmaking moved to Switzerland in 1969. Reference H69439931 pairs a compact 38mm matte steel case and black dial with the hand-wound H-50 caliber, a modified ETA 2801-2 deliberately slowed to 21,600vph and given a longer mainspring for an 80-hour power reserve, all on a khaki NATO strap. Its low price and genuinely manual-wind movement have made it a frequently recommended entry point into mechanical watchmaking.
